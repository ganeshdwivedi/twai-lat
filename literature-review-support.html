Literature Review Support in India

We help researchers structure literature reviews around themes, methods, findings and gaps instead of simple paper-by-paper summaries. Support can include source organization, synthesis tables, gap mapping and citation consistency.

Discuss on WhatsAppContact us

Scope

What this service can include

  • Search strategy and screening plan support
  • Thematic literature organization
  • Evidence and method comparison tables
  • Research gap mapping
  • Citation and reference consistency
  • Review chapter structure and transitions

Useful for

Researchers, postgraduate students, engineering professionals, authors and teams that need a clearer, more consistent document.

Final scope depends on the source material, document length and required format.

Workflow

A transparent six-step process

  1. 01

    Define the research question and review scope

  2. 02

    Organize the available literature and inclusion logic

  3. 03

    Group evidence into themes and methods

  4. 04

    Build comparison tables and gap notes

  5. 05

    Edit the narrative synthesis

  6. 06

    Check citations and references

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you help organize 30 to 50 research papers?

Yes. We can structure a literature matrix, group papers by theme and help turn the evidence into a coherent review.

Do you provide downloadable papers?

We can help identify legitimate sources and open-access routes, but copyrighted material should be accessed through lawful channels.

Can the review follow PRISMA?

Where appropriate, we can help structure a systematic review workflow around transparent screening and reporting requirements.
