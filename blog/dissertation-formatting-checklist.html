Dissertation Formatting Checklist: 25 Items to Check Before Submission

A dissertation formatting checklist for headings, margins, page numbering, contents, figures, tables, equations, references and final PDF review.

Updated 20 September 2026 · The Writing Agency India

Document setup

Verify page size, margins, font family, body font size, line spacing, paragraph spacing and page orientation. Confirm whether the university requires mirrored margins or a larger binding margin.

Use styles for headings and body text instead of manually formatting individual paragraphs. This makes large documents much easier to update consistently.

Headings and numbering

Check that chapter titles and subheadings follow one hierarchy throughout the dissertation. Do not mix numbering styles unless the university template explicitly requires it.

Ensure there are no orphan headings left at the bottom of a page and that spacing before and after headings is consistent.

Contents, figures and tables

Update the table of contents, list of figures and list of tables after final pagination. Confirm that every listed title matches the body text exactly.

Use automatic captions and cross-references when possible. Manually typed numbers are easy to break when sections move.

Pages, sections and front matter

Check Roman and Arabic pagination, section breaks, blank pages, headers and footers. Verify that front matter starts and ends where required and that Chapter 1 begins with the correct page number style.

Open the document in print layout and also export a PDF to catch line wrapping, figure movement and page-break problems.

References and final proof

Check that citation punctuation, author names, years, journal titles, volume information, page ranges and DOIs are consistent with the chosen reference style.

Before final submission, review the PDF page by page. A Word file can look different across systems, while the PDF is usually the actual layout seen by examiners and publishers.
