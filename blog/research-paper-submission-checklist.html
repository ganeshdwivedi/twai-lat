Research Paper Submission Checklist: What to Verify Before You Submit

A detailed pre-submission checklist for research papers covering journal fit, manuscript structure, figures, references, declarations and final quality checks.

Updated 20 September 2026 · The Writing Agency India

Confirm journal fit first

Before final formatting, read the journal aims and scope, recent papers and author instructions. Confirm that your topic, method and contribution fall within the journal's normal publishing range.

A technically strong paper can still be rejected early if it does not match the journal's scope or article type.

Check the title, abstract and keywords

The title should describe the study accurately without exaggerated claims. The abstract should usually state the problem, method, key quantitative or qualitative results and main conclusion in a compact form.

Choose keywords that reflect the actual topic and method. Do not repeat the same phrase unnaturally simply to target search engines.

Make the contribution easy to identify

Readers should be able to answer three questions quickly: what problem is being addressed, what is different in this work, and what evidence supports the claimed contribution.

State novelty carefully. Claims such as first, best or unprecedented should be used only when they can be verified.

Audit figures, tables and numerical consistency

Check that every figure and table is cited in the text, units are consistent, axis labels are readable and captions explain the content without forcing the reader to search the paragraph for basic context.

Verify that values reported in the abstract, results, tables, figures and conclusions do not conflict.

Verify references and declarations

Make sure every in-text citation has a matching reference and every listed reference is cited. Follow the required style exactly. Check conflict-of-interest, funding, data-availability, author-contribution and ethics statements where the journal requires them.

Do not cite papers you have not checked. Broken references and inaccurate bibliographic details reduce reviewer confidence.

Perform a final submission simulation

Open the manuscript as a reviewer would. Check whether the research question, method, results and limitations are understandable without needing information from a separate unpublished source.

Finally, export the required files, verify resolution and file naming, and compare the submission portal checklist against your prepared package.
