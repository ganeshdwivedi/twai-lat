Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of technical documents do you support?

We support engineering reports, research documentation, project reports, proposals, manuals, technical summaries and related professional documents.

Can you work with an existing draft?

Yes. We can edit, restructure, proofread and format an existing draft while preserving the author's technical meaning.

Do you guarantee approvals or publication?

No. We improve document quality and presentation, but final approval, acceptance or publication depends on the reviewing institution, journal or client.
