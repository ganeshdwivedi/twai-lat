Service terms and responsible-use principles

These principles describe the intended scope of our writing, editing and research support.

Scope

Work is performed according to an agreed brief, available source material and supplied formatting requirements. Authors and clients are responsible for checking the final document before use or submission.

No guaranteed third-party outcome

We do not guarantee university approval, journal acceptance, publisher acceptance, patent grant, search ranking or any other outcome controlled by a third party.

Academic and research responsibility

Researchers remain responsible for the authenticity of data, experiments, analysis, citations, authorship, ethical approvals and claims in their final work. Editing and documentation support should be used in accordance with institutional rules.

Copyright and permissions

Clients should provide only material they own or are authorized to use and should obtain any permissions required for copyrighted figures, data, images or substantial excerpts.
